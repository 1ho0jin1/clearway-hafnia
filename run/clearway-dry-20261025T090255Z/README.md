# XProtect alarms and bookmarks (F3), dry runs (2026-10-25)

`scripts/run_clearway.py --alarms --close-alarms-at-end` (the live service with the stand-in answering: no Hafnia
requests), then `scripts/report_clearway.py --xprotect`, which reads every alarm back from XProtect. The ledger
decides (raise, reopen, close; `clearway/ledger.py`), `clearway/alarms.py` carries it out through XProtect's REST APIs
on one thread, in order. Dry-run alarms are named "[dry run] ..." and their message says the stand-in answered.

## 1. Six incident cameras, 5 min (this folder)

| Camera | Alarm | Priority | Bookmarks | Snapshot | Final state (read back) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Breakdown | Clearway: STALLED_VEHICLE, Traffic operations | High | camera + lens_1 | yes | Closed |
| Bin | Clearway: OBSTRUCTION, Street services | Medium | camera + lens_1 | yes | Closed |
| E-scooter | Clearway: OBSTRUCTION, Street services | Medium | camera + lens_1 | yes | Closed |
| Gate | Clearway: TAMPERING, Security | Low | camera + lens_1 | yes | Closed |
| Tracks | Clearway: PERSON_ON_TRACKS, Rail control | High | camera + lens_1 | yes | Closed |
| Graffiti | Clearway: DAMAGE, Asset owner | Low | camera + lens_1 | yes | Closed |

6 alarms raised (one per incident, on its own camera), 12 bookmarks, 6 snapshots, 0 errors; the test harness closed
them at the end (6 of 6 read back as Closed). No reopen: the loops recur within 15 s, so each incident's alarm stays
open for the whole run, as designed (it closes once the incident has stayed cleared for 60 s). An XProtect action takes
3.6 s (median; max 11.8 s, the first ones fetch a token) on the alarm thread, which does not hold up detection. 13
questions, 6 cards, as the dry runs before.

## 2. Close and reopen: the bin, 1.5 min, auto-close after 5 s (`run/clearway-dry-20261025T090832Z-reopen`)

With `--close-after 5` the bin's loop clears for longer than the close delay, so the one alarm cycles:

| UTC | Ledger | XProtect |
|---|---|---|
| 09:09:37 | alarm | raised, bookmarks on the camera and lens_1, snapshot |
| 09:09:43 | close (cleared 5 s) | Closed, "Cleared: the spot has looked as before since ..." |
| 09:09:44 | alarm (reopen) | back to New, "Happened again ... (occurrence n)", bookmark and snapshot of the new occurrence |
| 09:09:57 / 09:09:59 | close / reopen | Closed / New |
| 09:10:12 / 09:10:14 | close / reopen | Closed / New |
| 09:10:21 | (run ended) | Closed by the test harness |

1 alarm, 3 reopens, 4 closes, 5 bookmarks, 0 errors; XProtect carried each out within about 1 s of the ledger's
decision (0.2 s median per action). Read back: Closed.

## Notes

- XProtect applies an alarm update about 1 s later, and reopening a Closed alarm (state New) works (probe,
  2026-10-25). `update_alarm` reads the alarm back for the fields it does not change, so a comment sent right after a
  close could reopen the alarm: the alarm thread names the state and priority in every update.
- Reasons for Closing are off on the hackathon machines; the close comment carries the reason.
- With Hafnia answering, the message starts with Hafnia's summary marked "[AI-generated by Hafnia VLM: check the
  clip.]"; not yet run live with alarms.
